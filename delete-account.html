Delete your account

My Gym Tracker by Sapko Builds

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open My Gym Tracker and log in.
  2. Tap the Account icon (top right).
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Trainers with Pro: cancel your subscription first, under Account → Manage billing in the web version. This makes sure you're never charged for an account that no longer exists. The app will remind you if a subscription is still active.

Option 2: by email

If you can't access the app, email mygymtracker.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email and password)
  • Your profile and display name
  • All trainer–client connections
  • Your trainer invite code, if you are a trainer
  • All workouts assigned to you or by you, including notes and completion history
  • Your subscription record in our database

Data is removed from our live database straight away. Encrypted backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten within about 30 days. If you ever paid for Pro, our payment provider, Stripe, keeps its own billing and invoice records for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
