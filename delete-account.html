Delete your account

My Gym Tracker by Sapko Builds

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open My Gym Tracker and log in.
  2. Tap Account (top right).
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

If you can't access the app, email mygymtracker.support@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email and password)
  • Your profile and display name
  • All trainer–client connections
  • All workouts assigned to you or by you, and every set you logged

Data is removed from our live database straight away. Encrypted backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten within about 30 days. We don't keep any data after deletion.
