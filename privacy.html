Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · My Gym Tracker by Sapko Builds

The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work: your email, your name and your workouts. If you're a trainer on the paid Pro plan, Stripe handles your payment and we never see your card. We don't show ads, don't track you across other apps or websites, and never sell your data. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from inside the app.

1. Who we are

My Gym Tracker ("the app") is made by Sapko Builds, based in Nepal ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, email mygymtracker.support@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressTo create your account, sign you in, and send account emails (confirmation, password reset).
PasswordTo sign you in. It is stored only as a secure one-way hash by our authentication provider; we can't see it.
Account type (trainer or client) and optional display nameTo show the right features and to show your name to your trainer or clients.
Trainer invite codeA random code each trainer shares so clients can connect with them.
Trainer–client connectionsTo link a client to a trainer after the client enters that trainer's invite code.
Workouts (titles, notes, whether and when each was completed)The core purpose of the app: assigning, completing and reviewing training.
Failed invite-code attempts (time only)To block code guessing. Removed after an hour or when you connect successfully.
Subscription details for trainers on Pro (Stripe customer ID, plan, status, renewal date)To provide the paid plan and its client limit. Your card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or store them.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health-app data or device advertising ID. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking software.

3. Who can see your data

  • You can see everything in your account.
  • Your trainer can see your email, display name, and the workouts they assigned you (including whether and when you completed them), but only after you join them using their invite code. You can leave a trainer at any time, which immediately ends their access.
  • Clients can see their trainer's email and display name.
  • Service providers that run the app for us (see section 5), only to provide the service.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data for advertising. We will only disclose data if required by law.

4. How we protect it

  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Database access rules are enforced on the server, so each user can only access data they are allowed to.
  • Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • Invite codes are random, can be replaced by the trainer at any time, and repeated wrong guesses are blocked.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify affected users of a breach where the law requires.

5. Service providers and where data is stored

The app runs on Supabase (database and authentication). Account emails are sent by Supabase or our email delivery provider. Payments for the Pro plan are processed by Stripe, which receives your email and card details directly. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments. Your data may be stored and processed on servers outside Nepal.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, connections, invite code and workouts are deleted immediately from our live database. Copies may remain in our providers' encrypted backups for a limited period (typically up to 30 days) before being overwritten. If you paid for Pro, Stripe keeps billing and invoice records for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Trainers with an active subscription need to cancel it before deleting their account.

7. Your rights and choices

You can, at any time:

  • Access and correct your data in the app, or ask us for a copy.
  • Delete your account and data in the app under Account → Delete account, or by following these steps.
  • Withdraw a trainer's access by removing them in the app.
  • Contact us about any privacy concern. We aim to reply within 30 days.

We handle personal data in line with Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018). If you live elsewhere, including the EU, UK or US, you may have additional rights under your local law; contact us and we will honour them.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the effective date above, and notify you in the app where appropriate.
