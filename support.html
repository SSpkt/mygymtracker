Common questions

How do I connect with my trainer?

Sign up as a Client and ask your trainer for their invite code. Enter it under Link with your trainer on your home screen and tap Join trainer. Your trainer can see your workouts only after you join.

I'm a trainer. How do clients find me?

Your invite code is on your home screen. Share it with your clients. If a code ever gets shared too widely, go to Account → Generate new code; the old code stops working, and clients who already joined stay connected.

My client says my plan is full.

The free plan includes up to 3 clients. To add more, upgrade to Pro from Account in the web version of My Gym Tracker.

"That code doesn't match any trainer."

Check the code with your trainer; letters and numbers are easy to mix up. After 10 incorrect tries you'll need to wait an hour before trying again.

I didn't get the confirmation or password-reset email.

Check your spam folder, and wait a few minutes before requesting another. Open reset links on the same phone where you requested them.

How do I mark a workout done?

Open the workout on your home screen and mark it complete. Your trainer sees it straight away.

How do I leave my trainer?

Go to Account → Leave trainer. Their access to your workouts ends immediately. Trainers can also remove a client from that client's page.

How do I manage or cancel Pro?

In the web version, go to Account → Manage billing. You can update your card or cancel there; Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Account → Delete account. If you have an active Pro subscription, cancel it first. See account deletion for details.
