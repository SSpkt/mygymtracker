Common questions

How do I connect with my trainer?

Sign up as a Client, then give your trainer the email you used. They send you a request from their app, and it appears on your home screen for you to accept.

I signed up as a trainer. Why can't I add clients yet?

Every trainer account is reviewed before it can connect with clients, to keep clients safe. You'll see "Awaiting approval" until then. Tap Check status to refresh.

I didn't get the confirmation or password-reset email.

Check your spam folder, and wait a few minutes before requesting another. Open reset links on the same phone where you requested them.

How do I log a set quickly?

Open a workout and use Quick log: type or use your keyboard's microphone, e.g. "bench press 60 kg for 8 reps". Or tap Repeat last to copy your previous session.

How do I remove my trainer?

On your home screen, tap Remove next to your trainer. Their access ends immediately.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Account → Delete account. See account deletion for details.
